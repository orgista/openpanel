# Environment assets

Active v0.3.39 pine-forest experiment:

`res/drawable-nodpi/skydome_pine_forest_fallback_v1.jpg`,
`app/src/main/assets/horizon_band_pine_forest_v1.glb`,
`app/src/main/assets/pine_forest_foreground_v1.glb`,
`res/drawable-nodpi/water_surface_pine_forest_v1.jpg`, and
`res/raw/forest_river_birdsong_background_v1.m4a` are the current generated
environment assets. The foreground GLB is packaged as a generated asset but is
disabled in `OpenPanelActivity` for v0.3.39 because the Quest 3S capture showed
its ground/cards reading as flat slabs in front of the creek.

- skydome_pine_forest_fallback_v1.jpg - active 8192x4096 equirectangular
  fallback dome for the pine forest pass. Generated by
  `scripts/generate-pine-forest-environment.py` from the local Poly Haven Pine
  Forest collection download at `/Users/macstudio/Downloads/pine_forest` and the
  panel-facing creek reference image at
  `/Users/macstudio/Downloads/43600e6a5e2d1b2de05000eb4bf89968e4613bc4.jpeg`.
  The reference image is a 1920x1080 forward render, not a true 360 panorama, so
  the launcher-facing arc is sharp and reference-matched while the side/back
  dome is intentionally a softer synthesized extension. The downloaded Blender
  scene is retained as source material only; the Android APK packages the baked
  JPEG/GLB derivatives, not the multi-gigabyte `.blend`.

- horizon_band_pine_forest_v1.glb - active forward 170 degree curved horizon
  shell. The embedded 8192x4096 JPEG is derived from the exact angular crop of
  `skydome_pine_forest_fallback_v1.jpg` and then lightly filtered. The previous
  0.3.35 pass rendered the band independently, which made the overlay look
  chopped/misaligned around the middle of the view. The aligned crop keeps the
  launcher-facing direction stable without using a 12K or 16K full skybox.

- pine_forest_foreground_v1.glb - generated local foreground detail, currently
  disabled at runtime. The GLB uses a compact embedded 2048x2048 alpha atlas
  derived from the Poly Haven forest ground, root, bark, fern, grass, and
  pine/fir textures. It was intended to give the headset nearby rasterized
  pixels and silhouettes, but the 0.3.38 Quest 3S capture showed the side ground
  pieces and vertical cards as visible slabs/posts. Do not re-enable it until
  the mesh is replaced with better-authored edge-masked geometry and verified in
  headset capture.

- water_surface_pine_forest_v1.jpg - optional subdued water base texture for
  future stream tests. `OpenPanelActivity` still keeps `ENABLE_WATER_BASE_LAYER`
  false by default, so the active motion remains the existing transparent shimmer
  planes unless a headset capture proves the base plane does not read as a slab.

- forest_river_birdsong_background_v1.m4a - 5 minute background loop derived
  from the local user-provided
  `/Users/macstudio/Downloads/Forest River Nature Sounds with Birdsong ｜ Gentle Stream Ambience in 4K for Sleep [EA7timbQpFc].m4a`.
  It was trimmed from a stable middle section, high-passed, low-passed,
  normalized for ambience, faded at the endpoints, and encoded as 48 kHz stereo
  AAC at 96 kb/s. Distribution rights for this YouTube-derived source must be
  confirmed before any public or ArborXR-promoted release; for a redistributable
  build, replace it with a CC0/public-domain loop.

Regenerate the active pine assets with:

`scripts/generate-pine-forest-environment.py --source-dir /Users/macstudio/Downloads/pine_forest --reference-image /Users/macstudio/Downloads/43600e6a5e2d1b2de05000eb4bf89968e4613bc4.jpeg`

Legacy lakeside environment:

`res/drawable-nodpi/skydome_lakeside_dawn_fallback_v1.jpg`,
`app/src/main/assets/horizon_band_lakeside_v1.glb`,
`app/src/main/assets/lakeside_foreground_v1.glb`,
`app/src/main/assets/lakeside_foreground_v4.glb`,
`res/drawable-nodpi/water_surface_lakeside_v1.jpg`,
`res/drawable-nodpi/river_ripples_v3.png`,
`res/drawable-nodpi/water_shimmer_fade_v1.png`,
`scripts/sources/nature.glb`, and `res/raw/forest_ambience.m4a` are
generated/derived:

- skydome_lakeside_dawn_fallback_v1.jpg — active 8192x4096 equirectangular
  fallback environment. Generated from Poly Haven's Lakeside Dawn tonemapped
  HDRI derivative (`scripts/sources/skydome_lakeside_dawn_polyhaven.jpg`),
  which is a real 8192x4096 panorama captured from a larger HDRI set rather
  than an upscale from the earlier 1774x887 concept pass. Poly Haven publishes
  the asset as CC0/public-domain content: https://polyhaven.com/a/lakeside_dawn.
  The full 8K tonemapped source is retained under `scripts/sources/`; it is no
  longer downsampled to 4K because headset review showed visible softness when
  the view drifted outside the forward horizon band. A 12288x6144 package
  target was tested on the attached Quest 3S, but it made the Spatial SDK
  launch path unstable/ANR-prone.

- water_surface_lakeside_v1.jpg — active 4096x2048 local foreground texture.
  Generated from an open-water band of the same Lakeside Dawn panorama and
  mirrored into a repeat-friendly tile for a broad, low-opacity horizontal
  plane. A closer lower-shore crop was tested on Quest and rejected because its
  rocks/branches plus the plane edge read as a floating foreground platform.
  The active crop avoids shoreline geometry, repeats at runtime, and pushes the
  water plane edges outside the visible lower field, while still giving the
  headset more local pixels than a distant skybox alone.

- horizon_band_lakeside_v1.glb — active forward 160° curved horizon shell.
  Generated by `scripts/generate-horizon-band-glb.py` as an 8,192x4,096
  embedded JPEG on a 38 m radius unlit mesh. The current 0.3.33 generator
  defaults use the local 16,384x8,192 Poly Haven Radiance HDR download, when
  present at `scripts/sources/lakeside_dawn_16k.hdr`, as a high-frequency
  luminance-detail plate while keeping the official tonemapped JPG as the color
  reference. This matters because a 160° crop from the 8K full-360 JPG only has
  about 3,640 real source pixels before resizing; the 16K HDR gives about 7,280
  source pixels for the same crop. The 360° 8K skybox remains as the fallback
  environment, while this narrower angular layer gives the visible launcher
  direction an 8,192-pixel display texture without fake over-sharpening.

  If the HDR source is not present, regenerate it by downloading the CC0 Poly
  Haven `16k hdr` file for Lakeside Dawn and verify MD5
  `bcfaac2566a740b64412fc24db62756f`. Then run:

  `python3 scripts/generate-horizon-band-glb.py --hdr-source /path/to/lakeside_dawn_16k.hdr`

  Do not package the raw 16K/24K HDR/EXR files in the Android app. They are
  hundreds of megabytes and are build inputs only.

- lakeside_foreground_v1.glb — rejected procedural local foreground. Generated
  by `scripts/generate-lakeside-foreground-glb.py` as Quest-safe geometry:
  shoreline terrain, rocks, grass/reeds, and dry branch silhouettes. It is not
  sourced from MetaOS or CGTrader; it is deterministic local geometry with
  vertex colors. Headset captures showed the v1 geometry could read as floating
  rocks/reeds or, after double-sided terrain changes, as a large flat slab.
  Keep it only as a reference for a later authored/Blender foreground pass.

- lakeside_foreground_v2.glb — previous textured shoreline foreground.
  Generated by `scripts/generate-textured-shoreline-glb.py` from Poly Haven's
  Coast Sand Rocks 02 diffuse texture at 2K (CC0/public-domain source:
  https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_rocks_02). The GLB uses a 112-vertex
  gently uneven shore plane with an embedded edge-masked PNG, `alphaMode=BLEND`,
  and an irregular transparent shoreline. It is positioned at
  `FOREGROUND_Y_OFFSET_METERS = -0.58f` in `OpenPanelActivity` so it sits below
  the launcher panel instead of reading as a dark band. 0.3.30/vc42 is the first
  headset-accepted v2 pass; earlier 0.3.29/vc41 proved alpha blending worked
  but was still too high/dark under the panel. v2 is no longer packaged in
  current builds; use the 0.3.30 artifact/audit if that exact checkpoint is
  needed.

- lakeside_foreground_v3.glb — previous 4K textured shoreline foreground.
  Generated by `scripts/generate-textured-shoreline-glb.py` from the same Poly
  Haven Coast Sand Rocks 02 source, but using the 4K diffuse download retained
  at `scripts/sources/coast_sand_rocks_02_diff_4k.jpg`. It keeps the same
  Quest-safe 112-vertex transparent shore mesh and runtime
  `FOREGROUND_Y_OFFSET_METERS = -0.58f`, with lower alpha and a softer mask edge
  than v2 so the added resolution does not reintroduce a hard foreground slab.
  v3 is no longer packaged in current builds; use the 0.3.31 artifact/audit if
  that exact checkpoint is needed.

- lakeside_foreground_v4.glb — active 4K displaced shoreline foreground.
  Generated by `scripts/generate-textured-shoreline-glb.py` from the same 4K
  Coast Sand Rocks 02 source. It keeps the v3 material/mask approach but uses a
  denser 73x30 grid (2,190 vertices / 12,528 indices) with subtle geometric
  displacement and triangle-averaged normals. This moves the foreground toward
  MetaOS-style real near-field geometry while keeping the composition
  restrained under the launcher panel.

- river_ripples_v3.png — earlier transparent highlight arcs authored in
  `scripts/water-ripples.svg`. Retained as source/reference but not active in
  the current headset build.

- water_shimmer_fade_v1.png — active transparent ripple/shimmer plate. It is a
  2048x2048 procedural RGBA texture with edge-faded alpha capped at 43/255 and
  about 29% sparse nonzero coverage. `OpenPanelActivity` uses it for two
  low-opacity animated water layers at 15 Hz and keeps the JPG
  `water_surface_lakeside_v1.jpg` base plane disabled. This is intentionally a
  subtle motion layer: previous captures showed the opaque/translucent JPG base
  plane could read as a rectangular slab, so do not re-enable that base layer
  without a headset capture proving the slab edge is gone.

- scripts/sources/nature.glb — stylized low-poly river valley (procedural:
  gaussian peak ring, winding river channel + lake basin, ~170 flat-shaded
  pines, vertex colors). Retained as an optimization/source experiment outside
  the packaged Android assets; headset captures showed it occluded the 8K water
  dome and introduced visible low-poly artifacts when the user looked down.
- forest_ambience.m4a — 30 s seamless loop cut from Wikimedia Commons
  "Birdsong mild sunny day.ogg" (public domain, no strings attached),
  self-crossfaded 3 s tail→head.
